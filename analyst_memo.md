## Slide 2 — Executive Summary

**Title:** April unwound March — and the PM funnel is finally working

**5 bullets (each WHY-driven):**

1. **ARR rebounded to $15.49M, fully recovering March's dip — but $150K shy of plan.** The March calendar effect reversed: leases that pre-paid in Feb's compressed cycle came back on the normal schedule in April. Reactivated leases jumped 50% MoM (6.3K → 9.5K), the cleanest signature of the unwind. YTD additional ARR is $1.78M vs $1.9M plan ($120K behind).
2. **Paid leases crossed 48% for the first time (48.06%, +1.13pp MoM, +4.48pp YoY).** Underlying engine is healthy: existing-paid retention grew (+2.8K vs Mar) and unpaid→paid conversions surged (+3.4K vs Mar). First-time paying tenants set a new record (15.1K, prev high 14.7K in Jan).
3. **PM-side took a major step forward: invite rate hit 85.6% on mature accounts (+11pp MoM, all-time high); onboarding accounts hit 71.3% (also a record).** This is the single most actionable positive signal in the deck — invite rate is the leading indicator that converts into next-month attach and activation.
4. **Tenant-side leading indicator weakened: portal attach rate on mature accounts dropped to 60.6% (vs 64.8% Mar, 70.2% Feb).** More invites went out, but a smaller share converted to attached portals. Concerning if it persists; a step in the funnel is leaking.
5. **Card volume hit a record $54.66M but share fell to 17.75% — lowest since Oct '25.** ACH volume grew faster than card. Underneath, charge-method stickiness erodes from 72% at M+1 to 65% by M+12, while ACH-first stickiness holds at ~81%+. Card is the smaller and stickier-leakier rail.

## Slide 9 — Card Volume Share

**Title:** Record card volume, but share at a 12-month low — ACH outgrew card

**Headlines:** Card $54.66M (record, +$1.9M vs Mar's $52.8M). Share 17.75% (vs 18.02% Mar, 18.24% Feb, 18.39% Apr '25). New $7.68M / Returning $46.98M.

**Trend:** Share has drifted down from 18.4% (Sep '25) to 17.75%. April is the lowest since Oct '25 (17.98%). 12-month range: 17.75–18.61%.

**WHY share fell despite absolute growth:**
- ACH volume: $239.99M → $253.32M = +$13.33M MoM (+5.5%)
- Card volume: $52.75M → $54.66M = +$1.91M MoM (+3.6%)
- ACH grew ~1.8pp faster than card → share mathematically falls
- New card $7.68M vs returning $46.98M = new-of-card share 14.06% (slightly up from Mar's 13.6% but below Feb's 14.27%)
- This is **ACH outpacing**, not card weakness. ACH growth is being driven by the mature-account onboarding + larger rent tickets concentrated in MidMarket/UpMarket.

**Implication:** Plan miss on card volume ($510K, ≈$120K ARR) is mix-driven, not adoption-driven. If ACH continues to outgrow card by ~2pp/month, card share trends toward 17.5% by EOY — that's the structural risk. The reduced-fee 2nd-cycle test needs to demonstrate it can shift ACH→card on the larger ticket sizes, not just the small ones.

## Slide 12 — KPI 1: Portal Attach Rate

**Title:** Attach rate softened — funnel has more invites but a smaller conversion to portal

**Headlines (KPI dashboard, lease-activation-month basis, 30-day window):**
- **April Mature: 60.58%** (vs Mar 64.83%, Feb 70.20%, Jan 68.04%) — **−4.25pp MoM, −9.62pp vs Feb peak**
- **April Onboarding: 39.01%** (vs Mar 51.80%, Feb 56.94%) — **−12.79pp MoM**

- Volume context: total new active leases hit a record (15.3K mature + 8.5K onboarding = 23.8K). More new leases entered the funnel than ever before.

**WHY (the non-obvious one):**
1. **Volume dilution:** Mature new active leases jumped from 14.6K (Mar) → 15.3K (Apr) and onboarding-account leases lag in attach. PMs took on a larger pool of new leases this cycle than in any prior month.
2. **Invite-to-attach gap widened:** Mature invite rate hit 85.6% (record); attach rate fell to 60.6%. Gap = 25pp, vs Feb's gap of 4.9pp (75.1% invite → 70.2% attach). **More invites are being sent, but fewer are converting to attached portals.** This is the single most important diagnostic on the slide.

**Implication / risk:** PMs sending invites is no longer the bottleneck. The bottleneck has shifted to **tenants opening invites and registering**. Investigate: invite-channel mix (email vs in-product), invite timing relative to move-in, and email deliverability/spam.

## Special asks

### 3 most non-obvious WHYs

1. **The PM/Tenant funnel asymmetry inverted.** Through Q1, attach rate (~60–70%) ran roughly *above* invite rate (~70–75%), implying many tenants self-registered without explicit invites. In April, invite rate (85.6%) leapt above attach rate (60.6%) for the first time. The funnel is now PM-led but tenant-conversion-bottlenecked. The squad's roadmap should pivot accordingly.

2. **The 48% milestone contains ~1.7K mechanical reactivation lift.** Reactivated leases jumped from 6.3K (Mar) → 9.5K (Apr). Baseline is ~6–6.5K. The ~3K excess includes ~1.7K calendar-unwind tenants (rough split: half the "Reactivated" excess is calendar mechanics, half is real reactivation from churned cohorts). Underlying paid % is closer to 47.6% — still up MoM, still a record, but the headline overstates by ~0.5pp.

3. **Card share decline isn't a card adoption problem; it's an ACH ticket-size problem.** New card volume held at $7.68M (matching Feb high). Card-tenant counts are stable. ACH grew $13.3M MoM vs card's $1.9M — ACH grew 7× faster *in dollars* despite tenant-count growth being more balanced. The squad's card-fee experiment treats this as adoption; the data says it's mix.

### Risks the headline numbers obscure

- **Paid leases 48% milestone:** ~0.5pp is mechanical (calendar). Reset expectations before May.
- **Invite rate 85.6%:** The downstream attach-rate softening is the more important story; don't let the headline crowd it out.
- **Card volume record:** Share at 12-month low. The $120K YTD ARR shortfall vs plan is structural to mix, not a one-month miss.
- **Churn 17.7K, "down from Mar":** Still ~2K above the Sep '25–Feb '26 baseline of ~14.5–15K. The new churn floor may have stepped up.
- **Activation 41% (April Mature):** A window artifact, but March's matured 68.1% is itself −6pp vs Feb. Real activation rate is softening modestly even after window adjustment.

### May pre-mortem (what could break)

- **Paid % drops to 47.5%** as reactivation reverts to baseline (~6K) — ARR could tick down ~$0.1–0.2M. Audience should be pre-warned this is *not* a regression.
- **Attach rate drops further (sub-58% Mature)** confirming the invite-conversion bottleneck. If yes, escalate to product as P0.
- **Card share at or below 17.5%** — confirms structural drift; revisit plan assumptions.
- **NSF rate spikes above 12-month band** — would suggest credit-quality deterioration, not just volume normalization.
- **UpMarket active leases continue to shrink** — could indicate concentrated PM churn at the top of the segment.
- **Invite rate falls back to 75%** — would suggest April's record was a one-off campaign effect, not a structural change.
